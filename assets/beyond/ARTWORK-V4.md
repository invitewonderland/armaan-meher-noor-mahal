# The Living Heirloom — original environment artwork

Tool: **Built-in image_gen**. No generative work or retouching was applied to the eight supplied couple photographs.

The selected source PNGs are retained in the workspace at `output/living-heirloom-artwork/hall.png` and `output/living-heirloom-artwork/garden.png`. Production assets are `assets/beyond/hall-v4.webp` (941 × 1672) and `assets/beyond/garden-v4.webp` (1052 × 1495). The WebP conversion preserves composition. These are imagined environments, not representations of an actual booked venue.
