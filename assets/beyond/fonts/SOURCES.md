# Typography

Bodoni Moda (normal and italic variable, optical size and weight) and DM Sans (variable optical size and weight), downloaded from the official google/fonts repository on 18 November 2026 and converted to WOFF2 with FontTools. No outlines or font metrics were modified. Both use the SIL Open Font License included here.

- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/bodonimoda
- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans

Noto Serif Gurmukhi remains bundled in assets/noor/fonts for the Punjabi dedication.

## Royal Atelier edition · 18 November 2026

Fraunces normal and italic, from the official google/fonts repository, replace Bodoni Moda in the active experience and exported keepsakes. Softness and wonk axes are fixed at zero for clear, consistent letterforms. Weight and optical-size axes remain variable. WOFF2 conversion uses FontTools; outlines are otherwise unmodified. The SIL Open Font License is included as FRAUNCES-OFL.txt.

https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/fraunces
